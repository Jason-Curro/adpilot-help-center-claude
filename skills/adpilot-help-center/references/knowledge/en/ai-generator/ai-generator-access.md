# AI Generator access and credits

## Who can use the generator?
- **Free:** AI Generator unavailable.
- **Starter:** up to **2 included AI generations per calendar month**.
- **Pro:** up to **20 included AI generations per calendar month**, plus eligible extra credits.

An AI generation produces a campaign proposal; it does not publish it to Meta. The app uses the selected Shopify product and your campaign inputs to prepare the proposal.

## Check your access
1. Open **Plans** to see your active plan.
2. Open **AI Generator**.
3. If blocked or out of credits, review your plan allowance before continuing.

Extra generation purchases are offered to Pro users; see [Buy extra AI generation credits](../plans/extra-generations.md). Credits and displayed availability may depend on successful Shopify purchase confirmation.

See [Generate an AI campaign proposal](../ai-generator/create-ai-campaign.md) for the complete workflow.
