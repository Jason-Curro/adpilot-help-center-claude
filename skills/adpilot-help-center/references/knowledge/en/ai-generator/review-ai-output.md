# Understand the generated campaign

## What the proposal contains
The generated plan can include campaign structure, suggested ad sets, audiences, creative ideas, advertising copy, budget allocation and performance indicators.

## Review checklist
1. Confirm that the product and business sector are correct.
2. Compare the suggested budget with what you can actually spend.
3. Verify every claim about the product; do not publish invented features, discounts or guarantees.
4. Check whether the proposed targeting is appropriate for your audience and advertising policies.
5. Adjust text and creative ideas before building ads in Meta Ads Manager.
6. Monitor actual performance independently once ads run.

**AI content is a proposal**, not an approved or launched Meta campaign. The app does not grant permissions to publish Meta ads automatically.
