# Generate an AI campaign proposal

## Requirements
You need **Starter or Pro**, available AI generations and at least one active Shopify product.

## Create the proposal
1. Open **AI Generator**.
2. Select an active product from the product menu.
3. Select **sector**, **country** and **advertising objective**.
4. Optionally enter a more specific business niche.
5. Enter a daily budget as a whole number between **1 and 10,000** and a duration as a whole number in the allowed range.
6. Select **Generate** and review the result after the request finishes.
7. Open the saved campaign detail if you need to see it again.

The generation may take some time. Do not repeatedly submit requests while one is running.

## Before using the output
Check the proposed audience, claims, text, budget and objectives for accuracy and compliance with Meta's policies. You must create the actual campaign in Meta Ads Manager manually.

See [Understand the generated campaign](../ai-generator/review-ai-output.md) and [Fix AI Generator issues](../troubleshooting/ai-errors.md).
