# Read optimization recommendations

## What the analysis is
AdPilot reviews uploaded performance data and produces a saved report with findings and suggested actions for ad sets and audiences. It can suggest maintaining, increasing, reducing or stopping a part of a proposed strategy.

## Review the report
1. Open **Optimization** and select a saved report, or follow the result page after an upload.
2. Check the period, uploaded report name and the campaign involved.
3. Review performance totals and individual recommendations.
4. Compare any suggestion with current data and your business targets.
5. Apply changes **manually in Meta Ads Manager** if you agree.

## Limitations
- AI recommendations can be imperfect and are not guarantees.
- AdPilot does not know subsequent live results unless you provide new performance data.
- The saved report does **not** itself change a Meta campaign.

For repeat analyses, see [When can I optimize again?](../optimizer/optimization-cooldown.md).
