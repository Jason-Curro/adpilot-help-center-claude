# Frequently asked questions: Optimizer and CSV

## Why is Optimizer locked?
Creating an optimization round requires Pro. See [How Meta Ads Optimization works](../optimizer/optimizer-overview.md).

## Can I upload an Excel file?
No, export a UTF-8 `.csv` with the required columns. See [CSV format and required columns](../optimizer/csv-requirements.md).

## Why are columns missing?
Your Meta export may use different headings or omit audience/interest or purchase-value data. Compare all 13 required categories. See [Fix CSV import errors](../troubleshooting/csv-errors.md).

## Can AdPilot improve my Meta ads automatically?
No. It recommends actions; you apply changes in Meta Ads Manager. See [Read optimization recommendations](../optimizer/optimizer-results.md).

## Why is the next optimization not available?
The normal user flow waits at least 72 hours between rounds for the same campaign. See [When can I optimize again?](../optimizer/optimization-cooldown.md).

## Can ChatGPT diagnose an ERR code?
A documentation-only assistant cannot access private logs. See [What does ERR-XXXXXX mean?](../troubleshooting/error-reference.md).
