# Frequently asked questions: getting started

## Does AdPilot launch my Facebook or Instagram ads?
No. AdPilot produces estimates, plans and recommendations, not live campaign changes. See [What is AdPilot?](../getting-started/overview.md).

## Can I use it without a Meta connection?
Yes for product evaluation, simulation and generating an AI proposal on eligible plans. Optimization uses a CSV that you export from Meta Ads Manager. See [How Meta Ads Optimization works](../optimizer/optimizer-overview.md).

## Does AdPilot modify my Shopify products?
The app requests read access to products; edit your listing in Shopify yourself. See [Review products with Product Ad Score](../products/product-ad-score.md).

## Which plan includes AI Generator?
Starter and Pro. Free does not include AI campaign generation. See [AI Generator access and credits](../ai-generator/ai-generator-access.md).

## Can I change the language?
Yes, select EN or IT inside AdPilot. See [Navigation and language](../getting-started/interface-and-language.md).

## Where is the official price?
Review the live amount, currency and conditions in Shopify confirmation before subscribing. See [Shopify billing: what to check](../plans/billing-questions.md).
