# Frequently asked questions: Simulator and AI

## Does a simulation spend my advertising budget?
No. A simulation estimates outcomes; it does not create ads or charge Meta ad spend. See [Create an advertising simulation](../simulator/create-simulation.md).

## Is estimated ROAS a guaranteed return?
No. It is an estimate based on model assumptions, not verified profit or future revenue. See [Read simulation results](../simulator/simulation-results.md).

## Why can I not create another simulation?
You may have reached the monthly quota for Free or Starter. Deleting a previous simulation does not restore quota. See [Fix simulation and quota errors](../troubleshooting/simulation-errors.md).

## What if the AI campaign contains a wrong claim?
Review and correct the proposed content before using it manually in Meta Ads Manager. See [Understand the generated campaign](../ai-generator/review-ai-output.md).

## If AI generation fails, do I automatically recover a credit?
The app attempts credit restoration on failures, but support may be needed for discrepancies. See [Fix AI Generator issues](../troubleshooting/ai-errors.md).
