# Review products with Product Ad Score

## What the score means
**Product Ad Score** evaluates the information available in a Shopify product listing, using criteria such as title, description, images, tags, pricing information and completeness. The total is shown on a scale from **0 to 100**.

This is a listing-quality indicator, **not a prediction of actual advertising revenue**.

## How to review your products
1. Open **Product Ad Score** in AdPilot.
2. Review the list of products and the overall score indicators.
3. Open or inspect a product's score breakdown, if shown.
4. Improve product content in Shopify if appropriate.
5. Return to AdPilot to see the updated evaluation.

AdPilot reads product information; it does not make edits to the Shopify catalog. Scores are recalculated when you open the page.

## Plan limits
Free shows up to **5** products, Starter up to **50**. Pro has no plan-coded scoring quota, but the current page retrieves at most **250 products per load**; it does not expose pagination beyond this. Read [Compare Free, Starter and Pro](../plans/plans-overview.md) for other limits.
