# Interpret a product score

## Score ranges
AdPilot uses four score bands: **80–100 Excellent**, **60–79 Good**, **40–59 Needs improvement**, and **0–39 Poor**.

The evaluation is based on six aspects: **title (up to 20 points)**, **description (25)**, **images (20)**, **tags (15)**, **pricing (15)** and **completeness (5)**.

## How to use the result
1. Start with products with lower scores.
2. Check whether the title clearly describes the product.
3. Make the description useful and informative.
4. Review image coverage and relevant tags.
5. Check the listed price, vendor and availability status.
6. Make edits in Shopify and re-open AdPilot to check the score again.

A higher score does not guarantee approval by Meta, conversions or improved sales. Prioritize accurate information rather than adding irrelevant text or tags solely to gain points.
