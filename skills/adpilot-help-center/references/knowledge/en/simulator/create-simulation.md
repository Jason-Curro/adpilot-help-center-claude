# Create an advertising simulation

## Before you begin
A simulation is a forecast based on assumptions, not an actual campaign. You do not need to connect a Meta ad account to run it.

## Create a simulation
1. Open **Ads Simulator**.
2. Choose **New simulation**.
3. Optionally enter a name.
4. Enter the **daily budget** as a whole number from **1 to 10,000** in the app's displayed currency.
5. Enter the **duration** in whole days; the current form offers **1–90 days**.
6. Choose the objective, business sector and country.
7. Select **Run simulation**.
8. Review the projected metrics and the saved result.

## Monthly allowances
Free: **3** simulations; Starter: **30**; Pro: no plan-coded monthly maximum. The allowance follows a calendar-month count, not necessarily your Shopify billing date.

## Important
The simulation does not spend money or create an ad in Meta. See [Read simulation results](../simulator/simulation-results.md) and [Fix simulation and quota errors](../troubleshooting/simulation-errors.md).
