# Read simulation results

## What the results show
After a simulation, AdPilot estimates campaign spend and advertising metrics such as **impressions**, **reach**, **clicks**, **CTR**, **CPC** and **CPM**. Starter and Pro also have access to advanced metrics such as **CPA**, estimated **revenue** and **ROAS**.

## Reading an estimate
- **Impressions**: how many times an ad might be shown; the same person may see it several times.
- **Reach**: approximate number of distinct people reached.
- **CTR**: share of impressions that produce clicks.
- **CPC**: estimated cost per click.
- **CPA**: estimated cost per acquisition.
- **ROAS**: estimated attributed revenue divided by advertising spend.

## Example
A projected ROAS of **2×** suggests an estimated 2 units of attributed revenue for every 1 unit of advertising spend, *not* 2 units of profit. Product costs and operating costs are separate.

**Do not treat forecasts as guarantees.** The estimate is not based on results from an active Meta campaign. See [Advertising glossary](../glossary/glossary.md).
