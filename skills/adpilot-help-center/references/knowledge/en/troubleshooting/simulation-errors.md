# Fix simulation and quota errors

## If a parameter is rejected
Check that the **daily budget** is a whole number from **1 to 10,000**, that the **duration** is a whole number in the range displayed by the form, and that you selected an available **objective**, **sector** and **country**.

## If the monthly limit is reached
Free includes **3** simulations per calendar month; Starter includes **30**. Pro has no plan-coded limit. The quota is based on simulations created during the calendar month, and **deleting a simulation does not restore a slot**.

## What to do
1. Review your plan on **Plans**.
2. Check the usage shown on the Dashboard or Simulator.
3. If the quota is exhausted, use the next calendar month's allowance or review the available upgrade options.
4. Avoid repeatedly submitting a form that clearly reports a limit.
