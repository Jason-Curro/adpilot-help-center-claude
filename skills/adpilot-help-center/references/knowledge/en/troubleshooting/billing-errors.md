# Troubleshoot billing and missing credits

## Before taking action
If Shopify checkout shows an unexpected **amount, currency or billing period**, do not approve the charge until you understand the terms.

## If payment was already attempted
1. Open the relevant Shopify billing information in your store admin.
2. Return to AdPilot **Plans** and review the current plan or credit balance.
3. Do not repeat the purchase merely because the screen has not updated.
4. Record the approximate time, plan or credit quantity, and the exact error/reference message.
5. Use the official support contact listed in AdPilot's current public information to request a review.

A public ChatGPT assistant cannot see invoices, card information, subscription status, purchases or application logs. **Never provide payment-card details, passwords or Shopify tokens in chat.**

See [Shopify billing: what to check](../plans/billing-questions.md) and [What does ERR-XXXXXX mean?](../troubleshooting/error-reference.md).
