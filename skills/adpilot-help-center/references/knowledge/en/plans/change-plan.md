# Change or cancel a plan

## Change your subscription
1. Open **Plans** in AdPilot.
2. Review the available Free, Starter and Pro options.
3. For a paid plan, choose the relevant plan and monthly or annual billing option.
4. Follow the Shopify confirmation screen outside the embedded app if prompted.
5. Read the price, **currency**, billing period and replacement terms before approving.
6. Return to AdPilot to check the active plan.

## Downgrades and cancellation
Changing from Pro to Starter is designed to take effect at the next billing cycle. Using **Cancel** to return from a paid plan to Free is a different operation: it requests cancellation and may change access immediately. Do **not** assume every downgrade waits until the end of the term.

## If checkout looks unexpected
Stop before confirming a charge. Check the currently displayed Shopify terms and contact official support if the plan, currency, price or billing status does not match what you expected.

See [Shopify billing: what to check](../plans/billing-questions.md) and [Troubleshoot billing and missing credits](../troubleshooting/billing-errors.md).
