# Buy extra AI generation credits

## Availability
The **Pro** plan offers one-time purchases of additional AI generations. The purchase form accepts quantities from **5 to 1,000** units per purchase.

The extra credits are intended for the AI Campaign Generator. Accepted purchased credits are available in addition to included monthly generations and are consumed before the included monthly allowance.

## Purchase safely
1. Open **Plans** with Pro active.
2. Find the additional generations section.
3. Select the quantity you need.
4. Continue to Shopify checkout.
5. Verify the **final amount, currency and terms** before approving payment.
6. Return to AdPilot and check the available generations.

## Important
Do not assume the purchase succeeded until Shopify confirms it. If the purchase or available balance appears incorrect, avoid repeating the payment immediately; consult [Troubleshoot billing and missing credits](../troubleshooting/billing-errors.md). No definitive unit price is quoted here until the currency configuration has been verified.
