# Shopify billing: what to check

## How billing works
Paid AdPilot plans and extra-generation purchases use **Shopify billing confirmation**. The Plans page offers monthly and annual options for paid subscriptions.

## Before you approve
1. Confirm the plan or generation quantity.
2. Read the amount and currency shown by **Shopify**.
3. Check the billing interval and tax information, where displayed.
4. Review the consequences of replacing or cancelling an existing subscription.
5. Approve only if the details match your expectations.

## Prices displayed on Shopify App Store (checked 17 November 2026)
| Plan | Monthly | Annual |
| --- | ---: | ---: |
| Free | Free | — |
| Starter | **USD $19/month** | **USD $190/year** |
| Pro | **USD $39/month** | **USD $390/year** |

Source: [AdPilot's official Shopify App Store listing](https://apps.shopify.com/adpilot), which states that charges are billed in **USD**. These are the *publicly listed* prices on the review date, **not an inspected checkout for your store**. Shopify's confirmation screen is authoritative for the amount, currency, taxes and terms you are approving.

Additional AI generation packages have a separate one-time purchase flow on eligible plans. Check their displayed total and currency at Shopify confirmation before approving; this guide does not assume your purchase was completed.

If a charge or currency is unexpected, stop before approval and use the official support channel linked from AdPilot's App Store listing or Shopify admin.

Never share complete card details, Shopify credentials or billing tokens in a public chat. See [Change or cancel a plan](../plans/change-plan.md).
