# Compare Free, Starter and Pro

## Plans and allowances
| Feature | Free | Starter | Pro |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Products shown for scoring | Up to 5 | Up to 50 | Up to 250 per page load* |
| Simulations per calendar month | 3 | 30 | No plan-coded limit |
| Advanced simulation metrics | No | Yes | Yes |
| AI campaign generations per calendar month | 0 | 2 | 20 |
| Create CSV optimization analyses | No | No | Yes |
| Buy additional AI generations | No | No | Yes |

*The Pro scoring configuration has no quota, but the current product screen fetches at most 250 items in one load without more pages.*

## Check your current plan
1. Open **Plans**.
2. Identify the plan marked as current.
3. Review the included features and monthly usage information.

## Published prices
As of **17 November 2026**, the [official Shopify App Store listing](https://apps.shopify.com/adpilot) shows **Starter at $19/month or $190/year** and **Pro at $39/month or $390/year**, billed in **USD**. Free is listed as free. Prices can change: review the actual checkout amount, currency and terms before confirming. See [Shopify billing: what to check](../plans/billing-questions.md).

CSV **export** is advertised in some plan text but is not verified as a usable app function; this guide documents the supported Meta CSV **import** only.

See [Change or cancel a plan](../plans/change-plan.md) and [Shopify billing: what to check](../plans/billing-questions.md).
