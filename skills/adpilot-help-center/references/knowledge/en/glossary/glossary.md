# Advertising glossary

## Advertising terms
- **Ad set:** group of ads within a Meta campaign, usually sharing a budget, audience and delivery settings.
- **Audience / targeting:** people you intend to reach with advertising.
- **Impressions:** total number of times ads were shown; repeated views count.
- **Reach:** estimated unique people who saw an ad.
- **Frequency:** average number of impressions per reached person.
- **Clicks:** interactions recorded as clicks for the selected report metric.
- **CTR (click-through rate):** clicks divided by impressions, typically expressed as a percentage. Example: 20 clicks per 1,000 impressions = 2%.
- **CPC (cost per click):** spend divided by clicks. Example: €10 spend / 20 clicks = €0.50 per click.
- **CPM (cost per mille):** spend per 1,000 impressions. Example: €10 for 1,000 impressions = €10 CPM.
- **Conversion:** desired result, such as a purchase or lead; the selected objective affects how results are interpreted.
- **CPA (cost per acquisition):** spend per acquisition.
- **CPL (cost per lead):** spend per lead.
- **ROAS (return on ad spend):** revenue attributed to ads divided by ad spend. Example: €200 attributed revenue / €100 spend = 2× ROAS; this is not profit.
- **Product Ad Score:** AdPilot's 0–100 indicator of product-listing completeness, not an ad-performance guarantee.
- **Simulation:** hypothetical campaign projection, not a live Meta report.
- **AI campaign proposal:** suggested plan that a merchant reviews and implements manually.
- **Optimization round:** saved analysis of an exported Meta Ads report.
- **CSV:** a plain-text table format that stores values in delimited columns.
- **UTF-8:** text encoding accepted for CSV upload.
- **Generation credit:** allowance to request one AI-generated campaign proposal.
- **Cooldown:** waiting period before repeating an operation.
