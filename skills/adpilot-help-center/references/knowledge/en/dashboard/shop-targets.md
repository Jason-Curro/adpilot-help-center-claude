# Set your store targets

## What store targets are
Targets are reference values for planning. They may include **sector**, **daily advertising budget**, **CPA goal**, **CPL goal** and **ROAS goal**. CPA means cost per acquisition; CPL means cost per lead; ROAS compares attributed revenue with ad spending.

## Set or change targets
1. Open the **Dashboard**.
2. Find the targets section and choose **Set targets** or **Edit targets**.
3. Select the sector; you may also specify a more detailed niche.
4. Add the financial goals that are meaningful for your store.
5. Save the targets.

Not every field must be filled in. Targets guide planning and AI suggestions; they are not promises or guaranteed outcomes.

## If a value is rejected
Check that numeric goals are non-negative, within the allowed input range and use a valid number format. Avoid pasting symbols or long text into numeric fields.
