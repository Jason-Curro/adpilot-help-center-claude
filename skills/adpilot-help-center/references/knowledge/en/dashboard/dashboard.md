# Understand the Dashboard

## Overview
The **Dashboard** is AdPilot's main overview. Depending on previous activity, it displays your store targets, plan and usage, progress through the Guide, recent campaign information and a recommended next action.

## What to do
1. Open **Home/Dashboard**.
2. Check whether your store targets are set.
3. Review your current plan and the available usage indicators.
4. Follow the recommended next action or use the navigation to open another feature.

The Dashboard can show different information for a new store and for a store that has already saved campaigns or optimization rounds. A missing recent campaign is not, by itself, an error.
