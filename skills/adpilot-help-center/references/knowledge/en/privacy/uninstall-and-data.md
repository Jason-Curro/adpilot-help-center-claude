# Uninstalling AdPilot and data requests

## Uninstall the app
App installation and removal are managed through your **Shopify admin**. If you decide to remove AdPilot, use Shopify's installed-app management interface and follow the confirmations shown there.

Removing an app is not the same as deleting an individual saved simulation or campaign. AdPilot processes separate Shopify uninstall and store-data removal events.

## When is store data deleted?
Shopify documents that the **shop/redact** privacy event is sent **no earlier than 48 hours after uninstalling** an app (and is not sent if the app is reinstalled before then). AdPilot is designed to delete its store-associated records when it receives and successfully processes that event. **This does not guarantee erasure at the moment of uninstall or exactly 48 hours later.**

For individual saved items, removal from the interface and underlying data retention are different; consult the current AdPilot Privacy Policy for the published retention commitment and ask the official privacy contact for store-specific confirmation.

Official reference: [Shopify privacy and webhooks](https://shopify.dev/docs/apps/build/compliance/privacy-law-compliance).

## Data or privacy questions
1. Review AdPilot's **current official Privacy Policy**.
2. Use the contact and instructions published there for relevant access or deletion requests.
3. Identify your store only through the official support/privacy channel; do not post private account data in a public AI chat.

Do not assume that every saved record is erased at the exact instant the app is uninstalled. If you need confirmation for a specific store, contact the official privacy channel.
