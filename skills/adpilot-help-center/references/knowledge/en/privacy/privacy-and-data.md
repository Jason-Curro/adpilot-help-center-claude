# Data, permissions and privacy

## Shopify access
AdPilot requests permission to **read product information** from the Shopify catalog. This is not a request to edit your Shopify products.

The app uses store targets and saved simulation/campaign information to provide its features. For CSV optimization, you provide a report exported from Meta Ads Manager; this workflow does not connect directly to your Meta account.

## Before sharing information
- Review imported reports for unnecessary private or personal data.
- Do not send credentials, API keys, Shopify tokens, full private CSVs or customer records to a public AI assistant.
- For legal details about data processing, retention and your rights, consult the **current official AdPilot Privacy Policy** rather than relying solely on this short guide.

A documentation-only ChatGPT or Claude assistant cannot read your store, see your campaigns, look up your billing details or act on your behalf.

Read [Uninstalling AdPilot and data requests](../privacy/uninstall-and-data.md) for removal questions.
