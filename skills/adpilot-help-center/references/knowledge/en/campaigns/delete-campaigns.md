# Delete a saved campaign

## Delete a proposal
1. Open **Campaigns** and select a saved campaign.
2. Select the available **Delete** action in its detail page.
3. Read the confirmation carefully before confirming.

Deleting a campaign in AdPilot removes the saved proposal from its ordinary view. **It does not delete or pause anything in Meta Ads Manager**, since AdPilot does not operate live ads.

Do not assume deletion restores used AI generation credits. If you still need information from the proposal, keep a copy before deleting it.

For privacy questions, see [Data, permissions and privacy](../privacy/privacy-and-data.md).
