# What is AdPilot?

## What AdPilot does
AdPilot is a Shopify app for planning, estimating and reviewing advertising campaigns for Facebook and Instagram (Meta). It runs inside your Shopify admin.

You can assess how complete your product listings are, estimate possible campaign performance, prepare campaign proposals with AI if your plan allows it, and review a Meta Ads performance report on Pro.

## A typical workflow
1. Open **AdPilot** from Shopify admin.
2. Visit **Guide** and set your store targets.
3. Open **Product Ad Score** to review the product listings.
4. Run a simulation with a budget, duration, objective, sector and country.
5. If your plan allows, create an AI campaign proposal and review it before using it.
6. After you have real performance data, eligible Pro users can upload a CSV report for recommendations.

## Important limitations
- AdPilot **does not publish, pause or edit Meta ads automatically**. Apply recommendations in Meta Ads Manager yourself.
- Forecasts are estimates, not commitments to deliver sales, traffic or a particular ROAS.
- The app cannot replace Shopify or Meta's own billing, account and advertising controls.
