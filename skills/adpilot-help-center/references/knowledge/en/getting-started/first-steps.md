# Your first steps in AdPilot

## Follow the in-app guide
Open **Guide**. AdPilot presents four activities and displays their completion status:

1. **Set targets**: choose your sector and, optionally, your daily budget, CPA, CPL and ROAS goals.
2. **Run a simulation**: select advertising assumptions and review the projections.
3. **Generate an AI campaign**: choose an active product and request a proposal (Starter or Pro).
4. **Run an optimization round**: after a campaign has real Meta Ads data, Pro users can upload a CSV report.

The Guide shows a progress indicator. It is an introduction, not a required installation wizard. You can visit other areas of the app without finishing it.

## If a step is unavailable
Some steps require a paid plan. Free users can still set targets, review products and run limited simulations. See [Compare Free, Starter and Pro](../plans/plans-overview.md).

For the first step, read [Set your store targets](../dashboard/shop-targets.md).
